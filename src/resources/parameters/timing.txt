- count (positiveInt, 0..1): Number of times to repeat
- duration (decimal, 0..1): How long when it happens
- durationUnit (code, 0..1): The units of time for the duration in UCUM, choose one of the following values: s | min | h | d | wk | mo | a
- frequency (positiveInt, 0..1): Event occurs frequency times per period
- period (decimal, 0..1): The duration to which the frequency applies. I.e. Event occurs frequency times per period
- periodUnit (code, 0..1): The units of time for the period in UCUM, choose one of the following values: s | min | h | d | wk | mo | a
- dayOfWeek (code, 0..*): If one or more days of week is provided, choose one or more of the following values: mon | tue | wed | thu | fri | sat | sun
- when (code, 0..*): code for time period occurrence, if provided. Choose one of the following values (the description is reported between brackets): MORN | MORN.early | MORN.late | NOON | AFT | AFT.early | AFT.late | EVE | EVE.early | EVE.late | NIGHT | PHS (After Sleep) | IMD (Immediate) | HS (Before Sleep) | WAKE (After Waking) | C (at meal) | CM (at breakfast) | CD (at lunch) | CV (at dinner) | AC (before meal) | ACM (before breakfast) | ACD (before lunch) | ACV (before dinner) | PC (after meal) | PCM (after breakfast) | PCD (after lunch) | PCV (after dinner)
- boundsDuration (Duration, 0..1): Length/Range of lengths, or (Start and/or end) limits

        - value (decimal, 0..1): Numerical value
